CardVault Support

Questions, problems or feedback: stratflight@gmail.com — we aim to reply within a few business days.

Where are my cards stored?

On your device, in the app's private storage. CardVault makes no network calls and has no server, so nothing is uploaded.

How do I move my cards to a new phone?

Settings → Back up. Keep the file somewhere safe, then use Restore on the new phone. This is the only way to move a vault — because nothing syncs, nothing is waiting in the cloud for you.

I deleted the app. Are my cards gone?

Yes, unless you made a backup. Deleting the app deletes its storage.

A scanned card came out wrong

Cards with uncertain readings are flagged for review. Tap the card and edit any field. Scans are more accurate when the card fills the frame and the lighting is even.

NFC tapping isn't working

NFC card reading needs an iPhone with NFC, and the card held near the top of the phone. Not all printed cards contain NFC — only ones that advertise it.

I bought the unlock and it's asking me to buy again

Tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen, and sign in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

Does CardVault sync between my devices?

No. There are no accounts and no sync. Use backup and restore to move a vault.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet.
